# DIRBE (COBE)

Docs/product table: https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/cobe/dirbe_prod_table.html

- 10 bands from 1.25–240 μm, approximately 1° resolution.
- Products include weekly/annual maps, zodi-subtracted maps, beam profiles, and point-source catalogs.
- Formats are commonly FITS and HEALPix grids.
- Suggested libraries: `astropy.io.fits`, `healpy`.
